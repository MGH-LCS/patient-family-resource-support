App Store vs TestFlight

Patient Family Resource app

What’s in the App Store version, and what’s only in TestFlight

Apple has approved a release. It’s a slightly older build than the one you have in TestFlight, so a few newer features aren’t in it yet. This page lists what each version has.

App Store Approved release Version 1.0.0 (26101401) · built October 14
TestFlight Latest test build Version 1.0.0 (26102302) · built October 23

Both versions get the same content: the same resources, pages and updates. The App Store version just shows some of it more simply, as described below.

In both versions

  • Setup when you first open the app: choose your hospital, unit and where you are in your stay.
  • Home screen of topic cards, each showing how many resources it has. You can turn the counts off in Settings.
  • Filtering by where you are in your stay, from Preparing to Come In to We’re Home.
  • Category pages with helpful information above the resources, such as café listings, checklists and tips.
  • Some cards go straight to one resource or PDF, such as the campus map.
  • Search across resource titles and the text inside them.
  • Essentials, a quick list in the search bar for showers, laundry, food, Wi-Fi, parking and more.
  • Saved resources, contacts, sharing and printing.
  • Short summaries under each resource, which you can show or hide.
  • In-app feedback.
  • New content arrives on its own, with no app update needed.

Only in TestFlight for now

These features will reach the App Store version in the next release. Until then, here’s what you’ll see in each version.

Subcategories on category pages

TestFlightEducational Resources has Breastfeeding and Trach care cards, and each one opens a page of just those resources.
App StoreEducational Resources shows one list of all its resources.

Pull to refresh

TestFlightPull down on the home screen to check for new content. A message says whether anything changed.
App StoreNew content still arrives, but on its own when the app is opened.

Phase tabs

TestFlightYour phase and “Everything” are tabs at the top of home and of each category page. Tap the phase name to change it. The timeline can be turned on or off in Settings → Display.
App StoreThe phase timeline sits at the top of home, and the phase chip in the search bar switches the filter.

Section headings on category pages

TestFlightFood & Dining is split into cafeterias, vending machines and food nearby, with chips to jump between them.
App StoreSame cafés and information, without those three headings.

“Open now” on cafés

TestFlightCafé cards show an Open now or Closed pill beside the name.
App StoreCafé cards show the opening hours, without the pill.

Phone, email and place cards

TestFlightPhone numbers and email addresses are cards you tap to call or write. Places, like the billing office, get a card with address, phone, email and website.
App StorePhone and email are still tappable links that call or open mail. Place details show as regular text.

Search shows each result’s category

TestFlightA small icon and category name sit above each result’s title.
App StoreResults show the title only.

Essentials goes straight to the answer

TestFlightEssentials opens full screen with a close button. Rows like Laundry or Visiting hours open right at the part of the page that answers the question. Visiting hours has a clock icon.
App StoreThe same rows open the page at the top. Visiting hours has a general info icon.

Spanish resource titles

TestFlightWith the app in Spanish, resource titles show in Spanish where a translation exists.
App StoreTitles stay in English. The resource text is still in Spanish where available.

Back to top on category pages

TestFlightA button appears on long category pages to jump back to the top.
App StoreNo button. Scroll up as usual.
If something looks different from what you expect in either version, send a note with a screenshot and say whether you were using the App Store or the TestFlight version.
