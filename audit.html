How our resources compare with MGB’s new website

Mass General Brigham rebuilt massgeneralbrigham.org. Much of the app’s wording came from the old site. We compared every resource written from an MGB page with what MGB says today: 28 resources against MGB’s current pages, and 8 more whose source page MGB has removed. 17 have at least one fact that no longer agrees. We also opened every link in all 76 resources.

Nothing in the app has been changed yet. Each resource will be worked through one at a time, worst first, so the app uses MGB’s own wording. Feedback is welcome on any of them, but there’s no need to read the whole report.
